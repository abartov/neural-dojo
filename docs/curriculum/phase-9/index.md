# Phase 9: AI Safety

**Duration**: 12-15 hours | **Modules**: 3

Evaluate, test, and secure AI systems. Build responsible AI that's safe, reliable, and aligned with human values.

## What You'll Learn

- LLM evaluation metrics and benchmarks
- Red teaming and adversarial testing
- AI safety principles and practices

- Build responsible AI systems
