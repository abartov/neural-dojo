# Phase 12: History

**Duration**: 4-6 hours | **Modules**: 1

Understand how we got here. The history of AI provides context for where we're going.

## What You'll Learn

- Key milestones in AI/ML history
- AI winters and summers
- The deep learning revolution
- The emergence of large language models
- Current state and future directions

## Key Insights

- **1956**: Dartmouth Conference - AI is born
- **1980s**: Expert systems and first AI winter
- **2012**: AlexNet reignites deep learning
- **2017**: "Attention Is All You Need" introduces transformers
- **2022**: ChatGPT brings AI to the mainstream
- **Today**: Rapid advancement in capabilities
